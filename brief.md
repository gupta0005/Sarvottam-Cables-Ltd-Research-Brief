# Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE) Research Brief

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd is a manufacturer of power cables, wires, and conductors. For Q1 FY27, the company reported a 35% increase in revenue to ₹1,428 crore, with net profit rising 6% to ₹82 crore. However, operating margins contracted due to a 14% rise in copper costs, a key raw material [Source: Business Daily Online, 2026-08-09]. The company is expanding capacity with a new plant in Bharuch, expected to start production in Q3 FY27 [Source: Sarvottam Cables Ltd, 2026-08-08].

## Bull case
- **Strong Revenue Growth**: Sarvottam Cables achieved a significant 35% increase in revenue, driven by robust demand from power utilities and data-centre projects [Source: Business Daily Online, 2026-08-09].
- **Capacity Expansion**: The upcoming Bharuch plant will increase cable production capacity by 30%, supporting future growth [Source: Sarvottam Cables Ltd, 2026-08-08].
- **Export Growth**: Exports now contribute 21% of revenue, up from 16% a year ago, indicating successful international market penetration [Source: Sarvottam Cables Ltd, 2026-08-08].

## Bear case
- **Margin Pressure**: Operating margins fell by 170 basis points due to rising copper costs, with price-variation clauses in contracts resetting with a lag [Source: Q1 FY27 earnings call, 2026-08-11].
- **Increased Receivable Days**: Receivable days increased from 78 to 96, mainly due to delayed payments from two large state utility customers [Source: Q1 FY27 earnings call, 2026-08-11].
- **GST Demand**: The company faces a ₹46.3 crore GST demand, which it plans to appeal, though it claims no material financial impact is expected [Source: NSE corporate announcement, 2026-09-02].

## Open questions
- **GST Demand Outcome**: The resolution of the ₹46.3 crore GST demand and its potential impact on financials remain uncertain.
- **Copper Cost Management**: How effectively can Sarvottam Cables manage copper cost fluctuations and pass them on to customers?
- **Receivables Management**: Will the company be able to reduce its receivable days and improve cash flow from state utility customers?
- **Promoter Pledge**: Although the promoter pledge has decreased to 4.1%, it was previously a concern at 35% in 2024. Monitoring future changes in promoter pledging is advisable [Source: Market Watch India, 2024-03-18; NSE shareholding pattern, 2026-07-15].
